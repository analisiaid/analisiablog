### 1️⃣ Better Not Using Last-Click Attribution on TikTok

 _TikTok recently partnered with[KnoCommerce](<https://knocommerce.com/>) \(a customer survey platform\) to [study marketing attribution](<https://www.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/beyond-click-attribution-views-impact-purchase>) on their platform._

Menggunakan data Click Source, TikTok hanya mendapatkan kredit sebanyak 21% atau -55% di bawah Search Channel \(Google Ads / Organic Search\) 😱 Kaget dong\! secara TikTok menjadi [discovery platform](<https://techcrunch.com/2022/07/12/google-exec-suggests-instagram-and-tiktok-are-eating-into-googles-core-products-search-and-maps/>) bagi young people.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/09/image-9.png)

Ternyata benar, data dari _post-purchase survery_ yang dikirimkan oleh KnoCommerce memperlihatkan angka yang berbeda. Kontribusi TikTok gak sekecil itu kok\!

They find out that **last-click attribution undervalues TikTok conversions by 73%** , while 79% of purchases that TikTok drives are not captured through common attribution methods.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/09/image-8.png)

Melihat temuan ini, TikTok memberikan tips dalam menggunakan Attribution Models di platform mereka:

  * Gunakan both **view-through & click-through attribution** models. TikTok menyediakan pilihan _1-day, 7-day, 14-day and 28-day click-through attribution windows, and 1-day, 7-day, and no view-through attribution \(VTA off\)_
  * **Use longer attribution windows**. Gunakan fitur [Performance comperison](<https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-attribution-analytics-performance-comparison?lang=en>) untuk membandingkan antar attribution windows di TokTok.
  * **Try a variety of approaches**. Jika memiliki resources, Marketers bisa mencoba metode lain seperti [marketing mix modeling](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marketing_mix_modeling>), [conversion lift study](<https://www.smartly.io/blog/what-is-conversion-lift-and-why-does-it-matter>) untuk mendapatkan _insight_ tambahan.

### 3️⃣ LinkedIn Ad Library: Brand Partnerships

Setelah lebih dari sebulan LinkedIn merilisi fitur ["Brand partnership"](<https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1627083>) label, mereka merilis fitur lanjutkan di mana Marketers bisa melakukan pencarian post dengan label "Brand partnership" di platform Linkedin.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/09/image-5.png)

Hal ini dilakukan karena undang-undang baru di [EU Digital Services Act \(DSA\)](<https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/digital-services-act-package>) mewajibkan Social Media Platforms untuk menghadirkan searchable database untuk _branded parnership contents_. Gak cuma LinkedIn kok, [TikTok](<https://library.tiktok.com/ads?region=GB&start_time=1664578800000&end_time=1694928452801&adv_name=&adv_biz_ids=&query_type=&sort_type=impression,desc>) & [Meta](<https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=all&ad_type=political_and_issue_ads&country=GB&media_type=all>) juga melakukan hal yang sama. 

_It is interesting, thoug_ mengingat kebanyakan _influencer campaign_ dilakukan di TikTok, Meta, & Youtube.

> Akses LinkedIn Ad Library: [linkedin.com/ad-library/brand-partnership-search](<https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/brand-partnership-search>)
