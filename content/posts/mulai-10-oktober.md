### 1️⃣ Google Demand Gen Campaigns are Out\!

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/05/image-35.png)

Sepertinya yang telah kita [bocorkan](<__GHOST_URL__/demand-generation/>) di bulan Mei kemarin, [Demand Generation campaign](<https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/demand-more-from-social-with-ai-powered-ads/>) \(a.k.a. Discovery Campaign Plus\) akhirnya bisa diakses secara publik setelah kurang lebih 5 bulan menjalani masa uji coba \(Beta\).

**Plusnya apa?**

  * New ad format \(**short-form video** & carousels\)
  * Add **product feeds** \+ videos + images + text 
  * Ad performance **report by ad format**.
  * Max **clicks bidding** option
  * X-funnel measurement: DDA, **brand & search lift** on YouTube
  * **Lookalike segments** to reach new customers



**TikTok, Meta Maju Lo Sini...**

Demand Gen campaign dirancang Google untuk menjadi tandingan Social App \(Meta & TikToK\) di mana banyak dipakai untuk _dicovery_ / _first-touch point_ sebelum melakukan pembelian.

![A graphic featuring three consumer insights around creator content consumption behavior based on a survey conducted by Ipsos.](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-uniblog-publish-prod/images/E02971641_Google_Demand_Gen_Blog.width-1000.format-webp.webp)

[Naranja X](<https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/demand-more-from-social-with-ai-powered-ads/>) \(Argentine fintech company\) yang bilang Demand Gen campaign cukup efektif dalam meningkatkan pengunjung website:

> When compared to paid social campaigns, Demand Gen achieved 3X higher click-through rates, at a 61% lower cost per action \(CPA\).

**Mulai 10 Oktober..**

Demand Gen campaign mulai rilis ke seluruh _advertisers_ & ditargetkan selesai awal tahun 2023.
