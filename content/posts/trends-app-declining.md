### 1️⃣ Google UAC +SKAN Is Coming

### 2️⃣ AI-generated Ad Copy by Microsoft Ads

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-13.png)

Microsoft memperkenalkan fitur untuk membuat _ad copy_ \(_headlines & descriptions_\) di advertising platform mereka. Fitur ini mendukung 35 bahasa dan bekerja dengan cara membaca konten di _landing page_ secara otomatis.

They said:

> Classified as Microsoft Confidential We’re folding the power of generative AI into how you create and edit RSA now, by recommending AI-generated headlines and descriptions for you.

**How it works**

  1. Click "Create new ad".
  2. Masukan final URL \(landing page\).
  3. Microsoft akan memberikan rekomendasi untuk ad headlines & descriptions.
  4. Accept their recommendations if you're happy with them.

### 3️⃣ Threads App Is Than Twitter, But...

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-15.png)

Dari studi yang dilakukan oleh [Website Planet](<https://www.websiteplanet.com/news/twitter-threads-research/>) terhadap 30 brands yang memiliki akun media sosial di Twitter & Threads App, didapatkan Threads app memiliki engagement rate lebih baik dari Twitter \(**0.45% vs. 0.02%**\).

> Engagement rate = \[\(\# of comments + \# of likes\) / \# of followers\] x 100%

Insights

  * Untuk news media, Threads app telah memiliki **1% of total followers** di Twitter.
  * **98% brands** yang dianalisa memiliki engagement rate yang lebih tinggi di Threads app dibandingkan Twitter.
  * **"copy+paste" content strategy** : most news media tend to make identical posts on Twitter and Threads.



**Is Threads App The Next Twitter?**

Laporan terkahir dari [Sensor Tower](<https://twitter.com/SensorTower/media>), Treads app mengalami **penurunan active users** sebanyak 🔻25% vs last weekend’s peak dan juga avg. [time spent](<https://www.cnbc.com/2023/07/13/meta-threads-engagement-has-dropped-off-sensor-tower-similarweb.html>) by 🔻50% \(from 20 minutes to 10 minutes\)

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-16.png)

**Latest Threads stats \([per July 13th, 2023](<https://www.theverge.com/2023/7/14/23795138/whats-next-for-instagram-threads-twitter>)\)**

  * 70+ billion impressions
  * 450+ million posts \(excluding reposts\)
  * 4.5+ billion likes

### 4️⃣ Repetitive Ads Damage Brand Reputation

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-17.png)

That's what we found from [a recent study](<https://magnaglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/MAGNA-Media-Trials-Nexxen-Its-All-in-the-Delivery.pdf>) by Magna IPG & Nexxen. Tidak hanya itu, ditemukan kecenderungan penurunan _purchase intent_ sebanyak 🔻16% dari pengguna yang melihat iklan lebih dari 6x.

VP of Intelligence Solutions at Magna [said](<https://magnaglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/MAGNA-Media-Trials-Nexxen-Its-All-in-the-Delivery.pdf>):

> The platforms are suffering too, because people also attribute the fact that this is happening to the platform. It really can be so annoying to people and viewers, that they’re willing to find content elsewhere

What can we do?

Regularly monitor the **ad frequency metric** on the ad dashboard \(_both Google & Meta have it already\!_\)

About [the study](<https://magnaglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/MAGNA-Media-Trials-Nexxen-Its-All-in-the-Delivery.pdf>)
