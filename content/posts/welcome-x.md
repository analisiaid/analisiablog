### 2️⃣ Rame di LinkedIn

Microsoft baru saja merilis [laporan tahunan](<https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/Investor/earnings/FY-2023-Q4/press-release-webcast>) untuk para investors di minggu lalu, di mana kita temukan dua informasi menarik dari laporan tersebut.

  * Jumlah kunjungan naik sebanyak +12% \(main feed +22% & public conversations +25%\).
  * Penurunan revenue dari produk Marketing Solutions \(ads\)

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-27.png)

Beberapa penyebabnya adalah; LinkedIn angkat kaki dari China, kondisi ekomni global, & pengurangan biaya maketing di tim internal LinkedIn.

But still, 23+ million users in Indonesia are [significantly higher](<https://www.statista.com/statistics/242606/number-of-active-twitter-users-in-selected-countries/>) than Twitter's users in Indonesia.

### 3️⃣ DSA & Display Campaign → PMax

Google Performance Max \(PMax\) again\! Kali ini Google memberikan pilihan kepada advertisers untuk mengganti Display & Dynamic Search Ads \(DSA\) campaigns mereka ke versi PMax secara sukarela. Yes, sukarela – no pressure & rush.

Few brands have seen the benefits of it, for example Shopee.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-28.png)

Santander mengonfirmasi hal yang sama, mereka mendapatkan hasil yang positif dari mengganti Display Campaign mereka ke PMax.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-29.png)

Fancy trying it? Learn more about it [here](<https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13780156>).

### 4️⃣ New Twitter, New Ad Tag

̶T̶w̶i̶t̶t̶e̶r̶ X sedang melakukan uji coba untuk menyamarkan label iklan di platform mereka 👀

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-32.png)[X News Daily](<https://twitter.com/xDaily/status/1684823323052490752>)

Seperti kita tahu, Twitter sedang sibuk melakukan re-branding ke brand baru mereka X. Mulai dari mengganti logo, merilis website x.com, pembaruan tampilan di App Store, bahkan [renovasi kantor](<https://www.npr.org/2023/07/28/1190895690/x-logo-twitter-building-san-francisco>) mereka di SF.

Balik ke penggantian label iklan, apakah perubahan itu tidak menyalahi aturan? Mengingat label iklan yang baru terlihat lebih kecil dengan warna soft-grey membaur dengan background colour. 

Federal Trade Commission \(FTC\) [said](<https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/online-advertising-marketing>):

> all ads must be ’clearly and conspicuously’ signaled in-app, though previous terminology referred to ‘clearly and prominently’

Is the 'Ad' tag clear enough? Here's what Google does:

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-33.png)

And Microsoft does:

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-34.png)

We think it's ok\! But obviously, we expect a higher engagement rate after X rolled out that new 'Ad' tag on their platform.
