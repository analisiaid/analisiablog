**Apa yang bocor?**

_Source code_ yang digunakan untuk memberikan _ranking_ dihasil pencarian Yandex. Ini menjadi hal yang menarik bagi praktisi SEO karena sampai detik ini, _Search Engine_ Google tidak pernah menjelaskan secara eksplisit bagaimana _content ranking_ bekerja.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/01/image-1.png)[source](<https://arseniyshestakov.com/2023/01/26/yandex-services-source-code-leak/>)

** _SEO Factor_ Versi Yandex**

  1. Age of links
  2. Traffic and % of organic traffic
  3. Numbers in URLS is bad for ranking factor
  4. A lot of slashes in URLS is also bad for ranking factor
  5. Gak boleh ada error 40x/50x \(i.e. 404 page not found\)
  6. Wikipedia slightly better score
  7. User behavior \(CTR, time on site, bounce rate\)
  8. Document age / last update
  9. Average domain position across all queries
  10. Crawl depth – jangan kebanyakan sub folder\!
  11. Backlink from main pages
  12. Number of search queries of our site/url
  13. Traffic from Wikipedia
  14. URL would be the last for search session
  15. Bookmarks
  16. Maps on page
  17. Keywords in URL
  18. Returning users
  19. % of CAPITAL LETTERS in title
  20. % of direct traffic
  21. Embeded video on the page
  22. Verified accounts on social networks
  23. Backlink anchors contain keywords
  24. Ratio of "good" vs "bad" backlinks
  25. Amount of advertisement on page – dear tribunnews\!
  26. Using Google Analytics
  27. Backlinks from the top 100 best websites by PageRank

### Sertifikasi Untuk Ahensi 

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/01/image-2.png)

Meta baru saja meluncurkan program [sertifikasi untuk bisnis](<https://www.facebook.com/business/news/meta-certified-company-program-launches-globally>), di mana akan memberi cara untuk memamerkan keahlian marketing Meta kepada calon klien. Program baru ini bertujuan untuk menyoroti bisnis yang stafnya telah menyelesaikan kursus di platform Meta, a.k.a _[Blueprint course](<https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/learn>)_.

As explained by [Meta](<https://www.facebook.com/business/news/meta-certified-company-program-launches-globally>):

> A Meta Certified Company is a company or organization that is recognized for their advanced expertise on the Meta Platform. For the first time, we’re offering Certification at the Organizational level, awarded to companies who achieve a threshold of individual Meta Blueprint certifications in specific focus areas. 

**Caranya gimana?**

Jadi minimal 20% karyawan harus telah mengambil course di [Meta's Blueprint](<https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/learn>), baru nantinya boleh mendaftar untuk sertifikasi _Ahensi._

### Akhirnya Bisa Negative Keywords di Google PMax

Meskipun banyak yang puas dengan performa [Performance Max Campaign](<https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10724817?hl=en>) \(a.k.a. PMax\), ada beberapa kegelisahan dikalangan para pengiklan. Misalnya, suka "nyerobot" iklan lain, gak punya banyak kontrol, gak bisa lihat _report by placement_ , gak bisa _exclude_ "brand keyword", dan masih masih banyak lagi 😓

Tapi tenang, kegelisahan itu akan sedikit berkurang dengan dihadirkannya fitur baru, [Account level negative keywords](<https://mobile.twitter.com/adsliaison/status/1619026730450313217>). Cuss update _brand keywords_ kamu\!
