### 2️⃣ SEO Heist Gone Wrong?

Remember this [viral tweet](<https://twitter.com/jakezward/status/1728032634037567509>) back in November last year? Jadi dia bantu [causal.app](<causal.app>) dapetin 3.6 juta traffic dari SEO dengan cara copycat konten Exceljet dengan bantu AI Tools.

![](__GHOST_URL__/content/images/2024/01/CleanShot-2024-01-07-at-09.47.21@2x.png)

_Of course, many people were not happy with it & some of them said that they were upset with Google for letting that happen._ Kok bisa konten copycat menggunakan AI dapet ranking di Search Result \(SERP\)?

**Karma has finally come**

 _According to the Ahrefs App, they lost most of their organic traffic since that viral tweet._

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GC7LML4X0AAyjU2?format=jpg&name=large)

[Semrush Ranking Factores report](<https://lp.semrush.com/rs/519-IIY-869/images/2024-Ranking-Factors-Study-ENG.pdf?version=0&aliId=eyJpIjoieThKaDhibCtQUFNucFpySSIsInQiOiJKZDAyU0ZVU1Njck9wN21jRHdGZENnPT0ifQ%253D%253D>) saysURL & Domain factors have the biggest influence to SERP position.
