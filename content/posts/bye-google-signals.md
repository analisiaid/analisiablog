### 2️⃣ Bye Google Signals From Reporting

Minggu lalu Google mengumumkan penghapusan penggunaan [Google Signals](<https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9445345?hl=en#zippy=%2Cin-this-article>) dari menu reporting di GA4 mulai [12 Februari 2024](<https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10976610?>).

**What you need to know:**

  * _It's**not fully removed** from GA4_. Hanya dihapus dari reporting, dan Google Signal masih berjalan & dipakai untuk fitur lain seperti [Demographic & Interest reporting](<https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/12948931?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid>), [Audience](<https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/12799087?hl=en>), and [Conversion](<https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9267568?hl=en>).
  * Pembaruan ini menjawab keresahan marketers terkait [_thresholding issue_](<https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9383630?hl=en>) di mana Google menyembunyikan sebagian data di reporting karena menyangkut _user privacy_.
  * _Jujurly_ , Google telah memberikan pilihan di menu Admin di mana marketers bisa [opt-out Google Signals](<https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10976610?hl=en&utm_id=ad>) dari menu Reporting.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/12/CleanShot-2023-12-09-at-10.11.43@2x.png)exclude google signals in reporting

### 3️⃣ Keys to Successful TikTok Creator Ads

TikTok baru saja menyelesaikan studi bersama CreatorIQ \(_creator intelligence platform_\) untuk mengetahui faktor yang mempengaruhi kesuksesan iklan di platform mereka. Total studi ada [26 halaman](<https://www.creatoriq.com/white-papers/tiktok-creator-ads>) tapi intinya sih:

  * _Grab attention from the start_
  *  _Foster a personal connection_
  *  _Show your product in action_
  *  _Use high-impact creative elements_
  *  _Close with a clear call to action_



**Some Practical Insight**

\[1\] Getting **attention from the start** is critical as 90% of ad recall impact & 80 of ad awareness impact is captured within the first 6 seconds.

\[2\] Ads with **Direct Speech Talking** Are 1.5x More Likely to Hook

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/12/CleanShot-2023-12-09-at-10.50.43@2x.png)

\[3\] 56% of Tiktokers say the **creator-driven Spark Ad** makes them trust the brand because the creator shared it.

\[4\] 71% of TikToker Say **Creator Authenticity** Led Them to Buy a Product

\[5\] Ads with **Text Overlay** Are 1.4x More Likely to Hook

### 4️⃣ Search Partner Network Can Be Opt-out Temporarily

Google membolehkan marketers untuk **tidak menayangkan iklan** di Search Partner Network untuk sementara waktu, termasuk PMax campaign 😱. Hal ini buntut dari l[aporan Analytics](<https://adalytics.io/blog/search-partners-transparency>) di mana website yang tergabung dalam Google Search Partner \(GSP\) tidak aman untuk brand, misalnya:

Iklan **CPNS US** tampil di **website Iran**

![](https://files.umso.co/lib_FcFBGbDutARPpNJY/e6uebxxzzya7ngbf.png)

Iklan **Data Engineer** tampil di **website porno**

![](https://files.umso.co/lib_FcFBGbDutARPpNJY/aites7ois4sbqsku.png)

Dan [masih banyak](<https://adalytics.io/blog/search-partners-transparency>) lagi.

Memang di awal Google [sudah menjelaskan](<https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722047?hl=en-GB>) mereka tidak bisa memberikan informasi website di bawah Google Search Partner \(GSP\) — _so it's at advertisers own risk 🤷‍♂️._

![](https://files.umso.co/lib_FcFBGbDutARPpNJY/caal0ptucd062sqw.png)

Untuk Opt-out Google Search Partner \(GSP\), Advertiser bisa [menghubungi account manager](<https://twitter.com/adsliaison/status/1732814876056191099>) masing-masing. Adweek bilang hal ini bisa dilakukan [sampai tanggal 1 Maret 2024](<https://www.adweek.com/media/exclusive-google-lets-advertisers-opt-out-of-search-partner-network-on-all-campaigns-following-adalytics-report/>).

### 5️⃣ Google Melakukan Pembasan Iklan Produk Finansial

[Mulai Februari 2023](<https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/14118861?hl=en&ref_topic=29265>), Google melakukan pembatasan iklan produk finansial di platform mereka, seperti:

  * **Tidak boleh mentargetkan** _gender, age, parental status, marital status, or ZIP code_
  * Berlaku untuk negara **US & Canada**
  * **Produk terdampak:** credit cards, home loans, car loans, appliance loans, short-term loans, banking and checking accounts, debt management products, banking products, financial planning, and financial manager services.



Google menghimbau advertisers untuk **meninjau ulang iklan** mereka sebelum dilakukan enforcement pada tanggal **28 Februari, 2024**. Akun akan ditutup oleh Google jika advertisers diketahui melanggar aturan baru ini.
