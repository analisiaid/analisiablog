### 1️⃣ Muda Kerja Bagai Kuda, Tua Kaya Raya

Bulan Augustus kemarin [US selesai](<https://www.wsj.com/economy/consumers/us-economy-seniors-spending-money-d9f529c5>) melakukan sensus penduduk, menariknya **penduduk usia 65+** di mana jumlahnya sekitar 17.8%, mereka memiliki **consumer spending terbanyak** 22% atau tertinggi dalam sejarah Amerika.

![](https://kanebridgenews.com/application/assets/2023/10/im-864652_600.png)

Loh kok bisa??

**\[1\] Pandemi Merubah Mindset**

Setidaknya satu juta warga Amerika meninggal selama pandemi covid19, hal itu menyadarkan mereka bahwa umur gak ada yang tau.

> “All my life it was, save for this, save for that, now there’s money in the bank and I’m spending in ways that bring me closer to friends and family than I did before.” said Maureen Green, 66, of Cape Cod, Mass. 

**\[2\] Living better, longer—and larger**

Kakek Nenek-nya lincah banget ya bun\! Kelompok umur 60+ makin banyak spending 2-3 tahun terakhir, hal ini dirasakan oleh American Cruise Lines di mana kebanyakan konsumen mereka adalah orang tua.

> “River cruising has traditionally attracted an older audience, and with more boomers retiring each year, we see both a rapid rate of growth and demand for longer experiences,” said Charles from American Cruise Lines

![](https://kanebridgenews.com/application/assets/2023/10/im-861957_600.png)

> “They’re riding e-bikes, they’re hiking, they’re traveling. And they’re doing these things for longer than they’ve ever been done.” - said [Circana firm](<Circana>)

**\[3\] The economy’s silver bullet**

Mereka kelompok umur 70+ memiliki 26% kekayaan Amerika, atau tertinggi sejak 1989.

![](https://kanebridgenews.com/application/assets/2023/10/im-861955_600.png)

**Apakah tren di Indonesia sama?**

Not sure, tapi trend pencarian "liburan bersama orang tua" mengalami peningkatan dalam 2-3 tahun terakhir – khususnya setelah pandemi.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/11/CleanShot-2023-11-26-at-09.01.22@2x.png)search trends for "liburan bersama orang tua"

### 2️⃣ Tokopedia x TikTok Could Be Good Move

TikTok dikabarkan akan [bergabung dengan Tokopedia](<https://katadata.co.id/desysetyowati/digital/6562ab0e402a3/tiktok-dikabarkan-gabung-tokopedia-transaksi-diramal-rp-610-triliun>), tentu supaya TikTok Shop bisa beroperasi lagi di Indonesia. _Is it a good move? We think so._

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/11/CleanShot-2023-11-26-at-10.18.58@2x.png)

**\[1\] First of all, Tokopedia desperately need help\!**

Their monthly visitors are declining over time, while Shopee is growing strong.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/F_2Z_V3XgAAcH9O?format=jpg&name=4096x4096)monthly visitors on similarweb

**\[2\] "Kaum Jompo" \(45+ y.o.\) lebih suka nongkrong di Tokopedia**

Beda banget dengan Shopee, di mana anak muda \(Gen Z\) banyak nongkrong di sana.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/F_2XZrOW4AESLIN?format=jpg&name=4096x4096)user's demographic on similarweb

**\[3\] TikTok Could Fill The Gap**

Dengan bergabungnya TikTok ke Tokopedia, diharapkan anak muda \(umur 18-24\) yang banyak nongkrong di TikTok perlahan mau mencoba Tokopedia.

### 3️⃣ Automated-created Asset \(ACA\)?

Belum lama ini Google merilis Automated-created Assets \(ACA\) di mana secara otomatis advertisers akan dibuatkan copywriting \(headlines & descriptions\).

**Catatan:** fitur ini _sebagai pelangkap_ Responsive Search Ads \(RSAs\) yang dimiliki oleh advertisers dengan tunjuan menambah incremental revenue & menghemat waktu kerja..

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/11/CleanShot-2023-11-26-at-06.35.43.gif)

I see...

  * Dari mana Google mendapatkan konten? dari landing page, existing ads, dan keywords
  * Cara cek performance di mana? masuk ke assets lalu filter “Asset source” IS "Automatically created”.
  * Kalau performance jelek bisa dimatiin gak? bisa banget.
