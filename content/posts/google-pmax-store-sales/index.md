### 1️⃣ GA4 Attribution Updates

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/06/Jun-24-2023-07-19-40.gif)[channels that can receive credit](<https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9164320?hl=en#062023>)

Total konversi dari Facebook Ads kecil banget di Google Analytics? Bisa jadi kerena Attribution Settings di Google Analytics 4.

Minggu lalu Google melakukan pembaruan untuk Attribution Setting di GA4, di mana sekarang bisa memilih bagaimana logic untuk memberikan credit ke marketing channels;

  * **Google paid channels:** _Only Google Ads_ paid channels can receive conversion credit.
  * **Paid and organic channels:**_Paid and organic channels_ are eligible for conversion credit, but only credit assigned to Google Ads channels will appear in your Google Ads accounts.



> Untuk melihat setting ini, buka menu the **Admin > Attribution settings** di Google Analytics 4.

### 2️⃣ Why Does Page Performance Matter?

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/06/image-15.png)

Kurang lebih 2 tahun lalu Google memperkenalkan [Core Web Vital ](<https://web.dev/vitals/>)sebagai salah satu **ranking factor di SEO** , including detailed report in Search Console.

Berapa besar pengaruhnya ke bisnis? Google berhasil melakukan studi dengan beberapa brands around the world untuk mejawab pertanyaan ini: 

  * _When a site meets the Core Web Vitals thresholds,[research showed](<https://blog.chromium.org/2020/05/the-science-behind-web-vitals.html>) that users were **24% less likely to abandon page load**._
  * _With each 100ms reduction in Largest Contentful Paint \(LCP\), the web conversion rate for[Farfetch.com](<https://web.dev/farfetch/>) **increased by 1.3%**._
  * _Reducing Cumulative Layout Shift \(CLS\) by 0.2 led[Yahoo\! JAPAN](<https://web.dev/yahoo-japan-news/>) to a **15% increase in page views** per session, 13% longer session durations, and a 1.72 percentage point decrease in bounce rate._
  * _[Netzwelt.de](<https://web.dev/netzwelt/>) improved Core Web Vitals and saw **advertising revenues increase by 18% and page views by 27%.**_
  * _Reducing CLS from 1.65 to 0 significantly**uplifted domain rankings** globally for [redBus.com](<https://web.dev/vitals-business-impact/#redbus>)._

### 3️⃣ Google Performance Max 👉👈 Store Sales

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/06/google-performance-max-with-store-sales.gif)[select store sales in pmax](<https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13653562>)

Sekarang Performance Max campaign sudah mendukung untuk store sales \(offline store\) untuk **reporting & bidding**.

Google [said](<https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13653562>):

> Store sales is an **online-to-offline ads measurement** solution that allows you to understand how much value your ads are truly driving for in-store purchases by using [your sales data](<https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10018944?hl=en>) in a privacy-safe way.

Key Features:

  * **Smart bidding** capabilities that optimize towards store sales conversions or both online & offline \(omni channel goals\).
  * Return on ad spend \(**ROAS**\) calculations for both online and offline conversions.
  * Reporting for **visit-to-purchase rate** by campaign.

### 5️⃣ Youtube A/B Testing for Thumbnails

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/06/image-19.png)[muaaz on twitter](<https://twitter.com/mws/status/1672018309426847744/photo/1>)

 _Thumbnail switching can be stressful and a very manual process? Say no more\!_

Minggu lalu Youtube mengumumkan**fitur A/B Testing** akan tersedia di Youtube Studio. Fitur ini membantu creators untuk menggunakan thumbnails yang berbeda di video mereka.

Beberapa creators sudah mendaptkan akses ke fitur baru ini, dan akan dirilis 100% oleh Google tahun depan.
