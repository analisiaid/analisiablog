### Branded Content Ads Is Now Partnership Ads

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/05/image-1.png)Instagram partnership ads

Introducing **[Partnership Ads](<https://business.instagram.com/blog/partnershipsads>)** by Instagram \(_formerly Branded Content Ads_\).

Itu loh, fitur Instagram di mana brand / business boleh menjalankan iklan \(_boosting_\) dengan melampirkan informasi "in partnership with ABC" \(_re: top left picture_\). Sekarang Brands bisa boosting / menjalakan iklan menggunakan tipe konten yang lain, seperti;

  * **IG Collab**
  * **Prodoct tag**
  * **@mention**
  * **@People Tag**
  * **Organic Post \(no tag\)**



Dari studi yang dilakukan oleh Instagram tahun lalu, Partnership Ads mampu meningkatkan CTR sampai dengan +53%, Biaya per akuisisi yang lebih rendah hingga -19%, dan 99% probabilitas untuk mengungguli Instagram Ads biasa \(BAU\). 

Deloitte has [said](<https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/technology/media-industry-trends-2023.html>):

> About 50% of consumers say UGC videos help them to discover new products or services to buy, and around 40% say they are more likely to purchase a product after they watch a creator review it

> Learn more about [Partnership Ads](<https://help.instagram.com/116947042301556>)

### New Ad Format Coming to Reels 

Di acara [IAB Newfronts](<https://www.iab.com/events/newfronts-2023/>) minggu lalu, Meta memperkenalkan 2 tipe iklan baru untuk platform Reels, yaitu;

  1. Augmented Reality Ad \(AR Ad\)
  2. Product Carousel Ad



![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/05/mta_AR_ads.png)

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/05/mta_AR_ads2.png)

Meta [said](<https://techcrunch.com/2023/05/04/meta-pitches-augmented-reality-to-advertisers-with-new-ar-reels-ads-and-facebook-stories/>):

> The addition of product carousels in Reels is in line with this _new commerce push, which is less direct, and less intrusive_. But could actually lead to a more organic, integrated in-app shopping experience.

Kedua opsi iklan baru ini akan tersedia dalam beberapa bulan mendatang.

Tidak hanya itu, Meta mengajak Brands / Businesses untuk memberikan _seamless purchase experience_ kepada users dengan menghadirkan fitur [Shops Ads](<https://www.facebook.com/business/news/expanding-access-to-shops-ads>) \- belanja tanpa harus keluar dari aplikasi Instagram / Facebook.

**2023 Timeline:**

  * Shop Ads akan bisa diakses oleh bisnis di US.
  * Uji coba fitur Shopping seperti _ratings and reviews, buyer email opt-in, and dynamic product pages._

### Youtube Shorts Ad is Now Available

Setelah meraih **1.2 miliar pengguna per bulan** , Google akhirnya membuka akses brand / business untuk menampilkan iklan di Youtube Shorts.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/05/image-7.png)Youtube Shorts Ad

Untuk saat ini, Youtube Shorts Ad hanya bisa diakses melalui iklan dengan objective **"Brand awareness & reach"** dan **[Youtube Select](<https://www.youtube.com/ads/how-it-works/set-up-a-campaign/youtube-select/>)** – produk untuk menampilkan iklan yang di _Top Premium Youtube Channels_.

As explained by [Google](<https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/new-ways-to-build-awareness-with-youtube-shorts/>):

> “With this update to Video Reach campaigns, you simply upload _a 60-second \(or shorter\) vertical video_ along with your other assets. If you’d prefer to use existing horizontal creative, YouTube’s campaign setup tools include AI-powered features that automatically adapt and optimize your videos for the vertical screen.”
