### 2️⃣ Disable Google Signals in Reporting

Minggu lalu Google melakukan pembaruan fitur [Google Signals ](<https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10976610?hl=en-GB&utm_id=ad>)di Google Analytics 4 \(GA4\) di mana _marketers_ **diberikan kontrol** apakah ingin menampilkan data dari Google Signals di menu _reporting_.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/10/image-6.png)

**What the heck is Google Signals?**

Google Signals adalah data sessions \(visits\) dari website yang terasosiasi dengan Google Account – misalnya pengunjung dari halaman Gmail.com, Youtube.com, etc.

**Why do we need Google Signals?**

Google Signals digunakan meningkatkan _tracking accuracy_ di website / app ditengah banyaknya pembatasan dari pemerintah & web browsers, seperti:

  * **GDRP** \(General Data Protection Regulation\): tracking users boleh dilakukan ketika ada _consent_.
  * **Browser Updates:** misalnya Safari hanya menyimpan data _cookies_ dari pengunjung sampai dengan 7 hari.
  * **3rd Party Cookies:** penggunakan _3rd party cookies_ atau _ad tracker_ dari pihak ketiga semakin ditinggalkan.



Tiga hal diatas berdampak ke _data collection_ di Google Analytics, misalnya banyak _conversion_ \(i.e. penjualan\) yang tidak teridentifikasi sumbernya \(i.e. Facebook Ads, Google Ads, etc\)

Jadi Google Signals dipakai untuk _estimate_ data yang hilang tersebut. _Does it make sense? Another problem is_.. ketika Google Signals diaktifkan, Google tidak mau menampilkan data di Google Analytics jika angkanya terlalu kecil \(_due to privacy_\) – seperti ini contohnya:

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/10/image-7.png)

Dengan memilih Include **Google Signals in Reporting Identify = Off** , data thresholding error seperti diatas akan **kecil sekali untuk muncul**. Benefit yang lain, Google Signals masih akan tetap berjalan – misalnya bisa mengirim**Audience data ke Google Ads**.

Fitur ini bisa diakses melalui:

  1. Masuk ke [analytics.google.com](<analytics.google.com>)
  2. Klik Admins
  3. Klik Data Settings
  4. Klik Data Collections

### 4️⃣ First Generative AI in Facebook Ads Manager

Seperti yang telah kita bahas di [newsletter bulan Mei ](<__GHOST_URL__/8x-lebih-banyak/>)lalu, Meta tengah melakukan uji coba fitur baru The AI Sandbox di Ads Manager yang memiliki 3 fitur utama: T**ext Variation, Background Generation, & Image Outcropping**.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/10/generative-ai-features-for-ads-manager-facebook.gif)[generative ai in ads manager](<https://www.facebook.com/business/news/generative-ai-features-for-ads-coming-to-all-advertisers>)

Meta bilang advertisers sangat terbantu dengan fitur tersebut, khususnya dalam hal **penghematan waktu**.

> “According to a survey of advertisers that participated in early testing, half of them estimate that generative AI will _save them five or more hours a week_ \- the equivalent of one month per year - noting they’ll be able to create multiple asset variations with the click of a button, reducing time spent between creative and media teams on time-consuming editing tasks and allowing for more strategic work.” – [Meta](<https://www.facebook.com/business/news/generative-ai-features-for-ads-coming-to-all-advertisers>)

Fitur baru ini sudah mulai tersedia di Ads Manager untuk beberapa advertisers dan akan bisa diakses **secara global tahun depan**.

Selain itu, Meta bilang mereka sedang mengerjakan AI tools untuk p**roduk messaging \(Whatsapp & Messanger\)** mereka:

> “Businesses will soon be able to use AIs for business messaging on Messenger and WhatsApp to engage with customers – helping with commerce, engagement and support, _unlocking instant conversational responses_. We’re testing with a small number of businesses in Alpha and plan to scale it further next year.”

### 5️⃣ USA vs Google Babak Hari Ke-16

Di persidangan antara Google vs US hari ke-16, ditemukan beberapa fakta menarik terkait Ad Auction di Google Ads.

_Here are a few interesting parts:_

  * _Google calls Ad Score as**Long Term Value \(LTV\) Score** , which isn't disclosed publicly and any ads with LTV<0 get thrown out. LTV Score is a function of advertiser's bid + ad quality \(copywriting / ad creative\) + landing page quality._
  * _Google use LTV formula to predict whether or not the benefits of showing a particular ad exceeds the costs. But when formula fails,**"pricing knobs"** come to play\!_
