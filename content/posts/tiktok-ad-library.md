### 1️⃣ TikTok Ad Library

Suka ngerasa kehabisan ide atau penasaran konten spesifik brand lain? Sebelumnya TikTok memberikan akses ke case study, namun baru-baru ini TikTok baru saja melakukan perbaruan halaman [Ad Library](<https://library.tiktok.com/ads?>) mereka, di mana sekarang kita bisa melihat banyak _insight_ dari iklan yang dijalankan oleh advertiser lain misalnya; _ad tergeting, regional reach, demographic, interest, etc_.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-20.png)

Ketika kita klik salah satu iklan, TikTok akan memberikan _in-depth summary_ dari iklan tersebut.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-21.png)

_It's better than Meta's Ad Library, isn't it?_

Sayangnya perbaruan ini baru tersedia untuk regional **Europe & UK**. _But we think learning from other brands in the West is still useful though._

> Access TikTok Ad Library: [library.tiktok.com/ads](<https://library.tiktok.com/ads>)

### 2️⃣ Tips Bermain Threads App Oleh Meta

Ditengah penurunan _Daily Active Users \(DAU\)_ di Threads App, Meta membagian 5 tips dalam meningkatkan _engagement_ di Social Media App terbaru mereka tersebut.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-25.png)[Threads DAUs are falling.](<https://twitter.com/wintonARK/status/1680990754838290433/photo/2>)

**5 Tips to Improve Engagement on Threads App**

  * **Make a splashy arrival by announcing you have joined the app’:** ojo diem-diem bae, kasih tau teman & keluarga kalau kita baru saja buat akun di Threads. 
  * **‘Start a conversation’:** ajak followers untuk ngobrol di Threads App, misalnya dengan buat status... "kalian time \#buburdiaduk atau \#buburdiminum guys? balas di kolom kementar ya\!" 
  * **‘Challenges and contests’:** \#giveaway adalah obat paling manjur ketika engagement lagi lesu. Coba buat kontes / challenges ringan tapi tetap menarik. Misalnya, "ARMY\! Dapetin gelas cantik berfoto foto Lisa dengan cukup melakukan FOLLOW @MyThreads, RT & LIKE tweet ini, & REPLY "MIN MAU MIN" + foto kalian lagi sarapan\! Good luckkkk yaww 🧡"
  * **‘Threads puns’:** buat permainan kata-kata, tapi tanggung sendiri risikonya yaa. Misalnya, "Spa apa yang bisa dimakan? Spaghetti" hehe
  * **Safety tools:** fun tapi tetap aman. gunakan safety tools dari meta, misalnya fitur [hidden words ](<https://creators.instagram.com/blog/introducing-threads?locale=en_GB>)🚫[ & who can @mention you](<https://creators.instagram.com/blog/introducing-threads?locale=en_GB>) 🔐.

### 3️⃣ Google Analytics 4 Updates

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-24.png)[Carly on twitter](<https://twitter.com/carly_boddy/status/1682162081729220608/photo/1>)

Ada yang baru lagi dari Google, sekarang [dimensions & metrics](<https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9164320?hl=en#072023>) untuk pembuatan [custom ecommerce report](<https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10445879?hl=en>) sudah tersedia di \#GA4.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-23.png)

Tidak hanya itu, Google juga menambahkan 3 metrics terkait revenue di [Explorations report](<https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7579450?hl=en#zippy=%2Cin-this-article>).

  * \[New\] **Gross item revenue** \(The total revenue from items only, excluding tax and shipping\)
  * \[New\] **Gross purchase revenue** \(The total revenue from purchases made on your website or app\)
  * \[New\] **Refund amount** \(The total amount from refunds given on your website or app\)
  * \[**removed**\] Ecommerce revenue.
  * \[**removed**\] Event revenue.



Untuk kalian yang memiliki report di GA4 menggunakan E-commerce revenue \(**removed**\), Google merekomendasikan untuk menggantinya dengan metrics: Purchase revenue. _Not urgent,_ Google bilang data dari Ecommerce revenue masih akan ditampilkan di _exisiting report_.

_Anyway,_ Google baru saja mengeluarkan **video series** untuk belajar Google Analytics 4 di Skilshop 🧑‍🏫 _[have a look\!](<https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLI5YfMzCfRtZNBRmhTEJkcHYvN_x_wpxM>)_

### 5️⃣ Few Marketing Facts From Duolingo App

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/07/image-26.png)

Tau aplikasi [Dulingo](<https://www.duolingo.com/>) kan? aplikasi gratis untuk belajar bahasa asing telah mencapai 20 juta of daily active users \(DAU\) 😱 Iya, udah sama kayak pengguna Twitter di Indonesia 😱 Berikut beberapa fakta menarik dari interview dengan [Emmanuel Orssaud](<https://www.wsj.com/articles/duolingo-advertising-tiktok-memes-5190560f>) – the company’s global head of marketing. 

  * 80% of its growth comes from word-of-mouth 🗣️.
  * The in-house team manages 80% of the content 🏠.
  * Replace TV Ads with Free TikTok Posts by Marketing x Virality Team 💃.
  * 50% of the marketing budget goes to performance marketing \(ad clicks, app download\), 50% goes to producing content & influencers 🤳.
  * Only running TV ads in markets where it sees a direct impact on new user growth \(Brazil & Japan\) 📺.
  * Mascot as meme strategy = having a mascot that has become a meme 💩.
