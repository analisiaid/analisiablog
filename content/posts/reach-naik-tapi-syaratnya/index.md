### 1️⃣ More Power for Creators on Meta

Meta merilis 3 fitur baru untuk mengoptimalkan konten & jangkauan \(a.k.a. organic reach\) yang dibuat oleh para Creators.

**A/B Testing**

![Meta Reels caption testing](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/imgproxy/_-FmT6dl2JE5eIWght1cWBI2UaSmhEhZvjATVfreNTk/g:ce/rs:fill:800:421:0/bG9jYWw6Ly8vZGl2ZWltYWdlL3JlZWxzX2NhcHRpb25fdGVzdDEucG5n.jpg)

Creators bisa membuat beberapa versi konten Reels dengan captions / thumbnails yang berbeda, dan dalam 30 menit akan diumumkan konten mana yang paling popular \(winning content\). Interesting, isn't?

**Achievements hub**

![Meta Reels achievements](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/imgproxy/A26lnB1GxGfRgisWrH21ry4adLbs3VFeI-7QdkRLWCE/g:ce/rs:fill:600:522:0/bG9jYWw6Ly8vZGl2ZWltYWdlL3JlZWxzX2NhcHRpb25fdGVzdDYucG5n.jpg)

It's a kind of gamification approach from Meta. Jika creator bisa menyelesaikan misi yang diberikan, Meta akan memberikan insentif \(misalnya more reach\).

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/11/Analisia-working-file-for-illustration-1.svg)

Meta juga fitur baru lain, seperti **Your Content dashboard** , **Highlights Card** , **New Reels Metrics** \(_reach broken down by followers and non-followers, distribution scores, and retention graphs_\) yang bisa dibaca lebih lengkap [di sini](<https://about.fb.com/news/2023/11/helping-creators-test-content-and-earn-rewards/>).

### 2️⃣ Welcoming Product Studio on Merchant Center

Buat bisnis yang memiliki produk katalog di Google, mereka baru saja merilis beberapa fitur baru menyambut hari belanja nasional 🛍️

**Product Studio**

 _Create beautiful products in seconds._ Deskripsikan gambar yang diingkan \(_prompt_\), and let's Google AI handle the rest.

![](https://digitalpress.fra1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/32x2gs0/2023/11/ezgif.com-video-to-gif--6-.gif)google product studio

Tertarik untuk [mencoba](<https://blog.google/products/shopping/google-product-studio-generative-ai-product-photos/>)?

**Updated Knowledge Panel**

Tidak hanya informasi lokasi bisnis, Google akan menampilkan lebih banyak informasi seperti _current deals, shipping and return policies, customer service information and ratings and reviews_ di Google Search Result \(SERP\).

![An image of a before and after of a panel of information on the right hand side of Search results about a merchant, which now shows ratings and reviews, promotions, online support pages, shipping and returns policy.](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-uniblog-publish-prod/images/Side_by_side.width-1000.format-webp.webp)

Punya promo menarik? Update [di sini](<https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14113862>)\!

**Label UMKM**

Google mulai menampilkan label baru di Google Shopping, apakah bisnis tersebut termasuk kategori _small business_ a.k.a. UMKM atau bukan.

Google said:

> Shoppers are looking for small businesses: In fact, 84% of people say supporting local and/or small businesses is important to them.

![A search for “shop kids foam blocks” on a mobile phone shows several results, including one that has a “small business” label.](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-uniblog-publish-prod/images/SRP.width-1000.format-webp.webp)

Fitur bisa diaktifkan di menu [Setting - Merchant Center](<https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12564247?hl=en&visit_id=638346801733061649-3985672911&rd=1>), atau Google akan mengkatifkan secara otomatis jika kriteria seperti jumlah produk, jumlah cabang, jumlah pengunjung, dst memenuhi.

### 4️⃣ Quality Score \(QS\) is Everything

[Jumat minggu lalu](<https://twitter.com/jason_kint/status/1718834031838560294>), United States Department of Justice kembali mempublikasikan bukti persidangan antara Google & US.

**Highlights**

  * Ad Quality digunakan Google untuk menentukan **ranking, disabling ad, broadening, display \(UI\), pricing**\([screenshot](<https://share.cleanshot.com/DFSVHxtD>)\).
  * [Quality Score \(QS\)](<https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167118?hl=en-GB>) berdampak ke min. bid threshold in any ad auction – **higher quality score = lower min. bid** \([screenshot](<https://share.cleanshot.com/KlFlH8FZ>)\)
  * **Landing page jelek = higher min. bid** threshold – bahkan sampai dititik bidding tidak bisa masuk ke auction \([screenshot](<https://share.cleanshot.com/Hh8jd2h4>)\)
  * Bukan SEM vs Paid Social, tapi **Google Discovery vs Paid Social** \([screenshot](<https://share.cleanshot.com/q614t7Rs>)\)
  * Google acknowledges that Social Apps like Instagram are **creating intent** and moving users **down the funnel.**



Total slides ada 126 halaman & bisa diunduh [di sini](<https://www.justice.gov/d9/2023-10/417378.pdf>).
