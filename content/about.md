Analisia is a performance marketing agency. This blog is where our team publishes the tactics, experiments, and hard-won lessons from running paid media for fast-growing brands.

Most marketing content is written to rank, not to teach. We do the opposite. Every post here comes out of a real account, a real test, or a real mistake — and we share the numbers, the setup, and the reasoning so you can replicate it without guessing.

We cover four areas: paid media strategy, analytics and measurement, creative testing, and the operational systems that hold it all together. No gated PDFs, no upsells — just the working knowledge our own team relies on.

## The team behind it

We are a small group of practitioners — media buyers, analysts, and strategists — who spend most of our week inside ad accounts. Writing is how we pressure-test our own thinking. If a tactic survives our publishing standard, it has usually already proven itself in a client account first.
